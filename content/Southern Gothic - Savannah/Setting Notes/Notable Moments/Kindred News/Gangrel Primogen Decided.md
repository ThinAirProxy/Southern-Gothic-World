**Issued by the Hand and Seal of Prince Marcus Devereaux**  

In the wake of MeeMaw’s descent into Torpor, both Malcom Grayson and Calliope Rivers presented themselves for consideration as the next Primogen of Clan [[Gangrel]].  

After thorough deliberation and private counsel, I have made my decision. As of this night, I formally recognise [[Malcom Grayson]] as Primogen of Clan Gangrel.  

This resolution is one of strength and clarity. Malcolm Grayson has long served the city with vigilance and restraint. His loyalty to the Clan and his unshaken commitment to the stability of the Court speak for themselves.  

I extend my respect to Calliope Rivers, whose service to the Circle of the Crone remains essential. Her attention, at this time, is rightly turned toward the matters that have arisen within her Covenant—matters of no small weight, and ones that deserve her full focus.  

Clan Gangrel remains a pillar of Savannah, and its representation in the Primogen Council reflect both its resilience and its future.  

**Prince Marcus Devereaux**  
*Khaibit of the Mekhet*  
*Bishop of the Lancea et Sanctum*
