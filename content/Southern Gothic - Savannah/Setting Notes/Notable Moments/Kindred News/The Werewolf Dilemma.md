***As told by Samantha Harrow***

As tasked by Sheriff Ryan Sheehan, I am providing a summary of the negotiation held beyond the Gangrel borders with the local Werewolf pack. The meeting took place in the deep swampland where their influence holds strongest. Tensions were high, but no blood was spilled.

Sheriff Sheehan spoke with strength and clarity, leaving no doubt as to where the City stands. I want to acknowledge the contributions of both Novus and Virgil, whose presence and words carried weight throughout the exchange. Their calm demeanor and ability to navigate the complexities of the moment helped guide the conversation away from hostility.  

The Werewolves have not agreed to restore the truce. However, they have made it clear they now understand the consequences of escalating the conflict. They see what we see—that the Brood is a greater threat to all. They may not care for us, but they care even less for being hunted by monsters they cannot control. As such, they have agreed to stay out of the fight. They will not assist us—but they will not move against us either.  

This outcome is not ideal, but it is progress. The threat of direct hostility from the Wolves has been put to rest, for now. We should remain watchful, as we will likely need to deal with them again in the nights to come. But with their claws pointed elsewhere, the City can turn its attention fully to the Brood.  

The war ahead will not be won easily. But one less enemy is still one less wound to bleed from. Focus your efforts. Stay ready.
