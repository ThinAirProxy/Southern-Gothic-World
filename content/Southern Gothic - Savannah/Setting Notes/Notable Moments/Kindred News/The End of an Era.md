**To the Esteemed Kindred of Savannah,**  

It is with a heavy heart that I write to inform you of a solemn development within our Court. Shortly after the setting of the sun on the evening of **Monday, April 7th**, **Prince [[William Ashford]]** was discovered by his loyal Ghouls to have entered **Torpor**. While the exact cause of this turn remains uncertain, we take some comfort in knowing there were no signs of violence or distress. His Ghouls, in accordance with long-standing protocols, have placed His Majesty in a **private, secured vault**, where he may rest without disturbance or risk.  

The Prince’s absence is a profound one—for centuries, he has shepherded this city through war and peace alike. Though we may each feel the weight of this change, I urge you all to remember that the strength of Savannah does not reside in any one individual, but in the unity and resolve of its Court.  

**The matter of succession is currently under review.** The **Primogen Council**, along with other senior members of the Court, will oversee the city’s night-to-night affairs in the interim. I encourage all Kindred to remain calm, vigilant, and focused on our continued stability, especially in these uncertain nights.  

If you have questions or require guidance, you may direct your concerns to myself or your Clan’s Primogen. We are here to serve the city—and we will see it through.  

In Loyalty and Vigilance,  
**Jonathan Hayes**  
Seneschal of Savannah  
Clan Ventrue
