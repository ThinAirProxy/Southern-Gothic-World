**Shots Fired at Hilton Head International Airport, Suspect Still at Large**  

*Savannah, GA — Authorities are investigating reports of gunfire at Hilton Head International Airport late last night. According to officials, multiple shots were fired near a private terminal, prompting an immediate response from airport security and local law enforcement. However, by the time officers arrived, the suspect had already fled the scene.*  

At this time, police have not confirmed whether anyone was injured or killed in the incident. No arrests have been made, and authorities have not released any details on potential suspects. Officials are urging the public to remain cautious, as the shooter is still at large.  

“We take any act of violence at a public transportation hub extremely seriously,” said a spokesperson for the Savannah Police Department. “We are asking anyone with information regarding last night’s events to come forward.”  

Airport operations were briefly disrupted but have since resumed normal activity. Passengers and staff are advised to report any suspicious behavior to law enforcement as the investigation continues.
