#### Prince
Minimum City Status: 6  
Reports To: No one  
Subordinates: All Court positions ultimately report to the Prince.  
Duties: The Prince is the supreme authority within the Court of Savannah. They set policy, interpret Kindred law, and appoint or remove all officials within the domain. The Prince’s word is law, but under Marcus Devereaux, this role also includes maintaining a functional chain of command, leading with clarity, and enduring the burden of final responsibility in all matters affecting the Court.  
Held By:  
* [[Marcus Devereaux|Prince Marcus Devereaux]]
---
#### Primogen
Minimum City Status: 5  
Reports To: Prince (advisory)  
Subordinates: Their respective Clan members  
Duties: Primogen represent the interests of their Clans and serve as an advisory body to the Prince. They may propose, debate, and with a majority, veto any of the Prince's decrees, providing essential checks and balances to centralized power. Their knowledge of their Clans, history, and local politics is critical in ensuring fair governance.

#### Seneschal
Minimum City Status: 4  
Reports To: Prince  
Subordinates: Librarian, Harpy  
Duties: The Seneschal is the Prince’s right hand and manages the Court’s daily functions. They schedule meetings, mediate disputes, and ensure all departments remain operational and aligned with the Prince’s vision. Should the Prince be unavailable, the Seneschal may act with temporary authority on their behalf.  
Held By:  
* [[Dorothy Abernathy]]

#### Sheriff
Minimum City Status: 4  
Reports To: Prince  
Subordinates: Hound, Deputies  
Duties: The Sheriff is responsible for enforcing the laws of the Prince and maintaining peace and order within the city. They direct all martial and investigative assets, responding to crises swiftly and effectively. The Sheriff coordinates with Deputies and Hounds to root out threats, enforce edicts, and ensure Court safety through active patrol and punitive measures.  
Held By:
* [[Ryan Sheehan]]
#### Deputy
Minimum City Status: 3  
Reports To: Sheriff  
Subordinates: Recruits  
Duties: Deputies serve as regional law enforcers responsible for overseeing a specific district or territory in Savannah. They coordinate patrols, investigate local problems, and uphold the Prince’s laws within their jurisdiction. Deputies are often the first line of authority Kindred will encounter when tensions rise in their domain.  
Held By:  
* [[Jacob Kleine]] - District 1  
* [[Samantha Harrow]] - District 2  
* [[Remember Whitlock]] - District 3

#### Hound
Minimum City Status: 3  
Reports To: Sheriff  
Subordinates: Fox  
Duties: The Hound is a covert enforcer who specializes in uncovering and neutralizing internal threats. Operating quietly and often independently, the Hound uses discretion and observation to resolve issues before they escalate. They act under the Sheriff’s direction but are entrusted with a level of autonomy due to the delicate nature of their work.

#### Fox

Subordinates: None

They are tasked with monitoring minor disturbances, identifying emerging threats, and learning how to operate without drawing attention. Subordinates: Keeper of Elysium, Master of the Watch  
Duties: The Vizir is the cultural and ceremonial steward of the Court. They oversee tradition, manage Elysia, and advise on spiritual matters. As an anchor of decorum and discipline, the Vizir ensures that Court rituals and customs are upheld.
