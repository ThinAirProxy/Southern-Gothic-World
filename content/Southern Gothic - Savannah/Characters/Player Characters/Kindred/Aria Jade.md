*Aria's mass and stature make her an imposing presence wherever she goes. Standing over 8 feet tall and weighing over 700lbs, she certainly draws attention.*  

***Description:***  
Aria is a massive figure that moves with compelling fluidity, carrying her immense layers of lean muscle and adipose with dignified grace often dressed in well-tailored garments – nevermind that many of her day-to-day looks are re-styled pieces. She is well-kempt, with long hair always styled and face warmly painted to accentuate her features and accompany whatever outfit she has on. Aria is a frequent background figure at populous social events, though her near-perfect smile and distinct chortle at times draw attention to her if her towering stature fails to. She is personable and articulate.  

***Personality:***  
An ally easily made and an enemy difficult to shake. Aria is prone to holding grudges and executing petty revenge. She is generally well-composed – a pot slow to boil but explosive once rolling. Aria is precise, tactical, and calculating. Every conversation is a vie for power, a tool used to forge a path forward in her unknown motivations.
