***Description:***  
Toni is a short, strikingly curvy woman with a big personality and zero shame in how she presents herself. She dresses in revealing, body-hugging outfits that highlight her form and make no apologies for her confidence. Her mess of bright red curls is rarely tamed, giving her a look that’s half siren, half street brawler. Whether she’s in boots and mesh at Club Jericho or in a torn-up leather jacket conducting Rook business, Toni never looks out of place, she *is* the place.

***Personality:***  
Fast-talking, flippant, and razor-sharp, Toni mixes Brooklyn attitude with street-born strategy. She throws insults like punches and praise like bribes, quick, heavy, and never free. She’s clinically depressed but owns it with dark humor and deadpan charm. A connoisseur of pleasure in all its forms, Toni indulges in memes, sex, music, and the subtle chemical echoes of antidepressants in the blood she feeds on. Beneath her ruthless pragmatism is a hunger for control, loyalty, and something like love—but she’d never call it that.
