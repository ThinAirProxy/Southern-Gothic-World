***Description:*** Roxanne Virelli looks like someone who’s fought to stay on her feet more than once—and didn’t always win. She’s got a kind of tousled magnetism: shoulder-length, dirty blonde hair that lives in a permanent state of disheveled defiance, and tired blue eyes that flicker between scorn and sorrow. Her face is expressive, all furrowed brows and sharp glances, and there’s a faded scar near her left cheekbone—evidence of a night she doesn’t talk about.  

She wears layered clothes like armor: threadbare tees under oversized jackets, ripped jeans, and scuffed boots that look like they’ve seen more action than she’ll ever admit. There’s a cigarette burn on the sleeve of her coat and always a flask somewhere on her person. She moves like someone who used to care about appearances and now just wants to get through the room without a scene—unless you ask for one.  

***Personality:*** Roxanne Virelli is the kind of person who meets every new situation with a sigh, a dry quip, and a readiness to throw the first punch—verbally or otherwise. She wears cynicism like a second skin, a shield forged from every betrayal, every cold case, and every body the system forgot. She’s blunt to the point of abrasiveness, allergic to bullshit, and keeps people at a distance.  
She doesn’t trust easily, doesn’t love gently, and doesn’t forgive often—but if you earn her loyalty, it’s yours until the bitter end.
