***Description:***  
Lawrence Hart carries himself cautiously, all 4’9 of him lined with a visible landscape of muscle when he is not swallowed by oversized hoodies and baggy jeans. Layers of golden-brown curls frame his face haphazardly, the longest of the locks brushing against his shoulders. The brown shadow of facial hair that ghosts his squared jawline barely conceals acne scars and scabs, though they would be less noticeable if he wasn't constantly picking at them.

His resting bitch face occasionally dissolves to reveal the doe-eyed portrait of naivety hiding underneath. When he speaks, which is notably infrequent outside of his work engagements, a heavy southern drawl falls out in a pressured mumble that leaves most folks asking him to either ‘speak up’ or ‘slow down’.

Lawrence stays busy juggling school, paid internship, and volunteer work – leaving very little time to socialize outside of those obligations. He is often noticeably sweaty, though he keeps himself well-groomed and rarely smells worse than a sweaty horse stable. He has an air like he may be persistently on edge, eyes darting around as if on the lookout for a predator.  

***Personality:***  
Lawrence is reserved, kind-natured, and physically unpredictable. He struggles with expressing himself verbally, often turning to journaling and refusing to open up to the people around him. If agitated enough, he is prone to swinging first and running next – his main method of ‘diffusing’ a situation in which he wishes no longer to partake.  

In lieu of deep connections, Lawrence surrounds himself with acquaintances who prefer to tout their own accomplishments in pissing contests with others. This draws the limelight away from him, which he greatly prefers. He lives to serve others, his prominent solace found in his usefulness. A deeply insecure man about the confusing and tumultuous emotions within him, Lawrence values the praise and appreciation he receives from others – even when it doesn't feel deserved.
