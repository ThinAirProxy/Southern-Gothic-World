***Description:***   
Sadie is a waifish figure, hunched and hollow-eyed, her body bearing the marks of too many years spent surviving rather than living. Her skin is pale, almost sallow, stretched thin over sharp bones and old scarts with dirt ground into the creases of her hands and under nails. Her long brown hair hands in matted tangles, wild and unbrushed, half-obscuring her face etched with exhaustion and feral wariness. Her clothes are torn, stained and clinging loosely to her frame, flecked with mud, dried blood and the grit of life spent hiding in the margins.   

***Personality:***  
Sadie moves like a creature half-feral, started by her own thoughts or even the slightest noise, eyes darting often with restless paranoia that flicker between lucidity and a haunted distance. She is a fractured soul shaped by loss, isolation and the slow erosion of sanity. Once described as both strong and sharp, she remains now a husk of the person she used to be. She swings between moments of lucidity and wild animalistic panic. She may spend hours just staring at nothing, and another growling at a shadow that moves too close.
