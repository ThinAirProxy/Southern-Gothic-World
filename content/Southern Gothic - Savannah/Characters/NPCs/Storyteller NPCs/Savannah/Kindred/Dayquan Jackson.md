***Description:***  
Dayquan stands a little over six feet tall with a naturally athletic build—lean more than bulky, all long limbs and restless energy. His movements carry the easy fluidity of someone who's always in motion, whether climbing, sprinting, or bouncing in place when he's excited. His skin is a warm brown, smooth and now permanently marked by the minor scrapes and bruises from parkour and other ill-advised stunts from when he was alive.  

His hair is short and cleanly faded on the sides, his expressive dark eyes are quick to narrow with suspicion or light up with curiosity, and his smile is wide, charming, and just a little crooked—like he's about to say something he knows he probably shouldn't.  

He favors layered streetwear and athletic gear: joggers, oversized hoodies, scuffed-up sneakers, and often a beat-up backpack with too many keychains and an old patch sewn onto the strap that he swears isn't a sigil, just looks cool. There's usually a bracelet or charm on him that looks handmade—leather cords, bones, feathers, something weird he insists has *energy*.  

***Personality:***  
Dayquan is bold, clever, and absolutely the kind of guy who dives in headfirst without reading the instructions. He's quick to laugh, quicker to talk, and has a habit of masking nervousness with bravado.  He's curious in the way people are just before they burn themselves, drawn to danger and mystery like they're offering him a personal invitation. While he comes off cocky or unserious, there's a real hunger to understand the strange world he's found himself in—and a stubborn refusal to be left behind.  

He's impulsive but not unkind, loyal to people who give him a shot, and deeply fascinated by magic, spirits, and the unknown. His interest in witchcraft started as a joke and turned into something that nearly got him killed. Now, he's trying to figure out what it means to be a vampire when you didn't exactly ask for it, didn't plan for it, and still think it's sick as hell.
