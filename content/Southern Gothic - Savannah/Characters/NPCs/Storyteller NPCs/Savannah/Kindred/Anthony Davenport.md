***Description:***  
Anthony Davenport is not a man who can keep to the shadows, try as he might. With his massive frame and the dense bulk he carries, he looms over everyone in any room and exerts a pressure that many shy away from. He is pale-skinned, though years of sunwear show in the lines and pock marks set deeply into his face. His dark hair is neatly cropped and usually styled simply, and the small diamond studs he wears in his ears are the only modification to his body from the mortal coil.  

He is primarily seen in a black-and-white suit that may or may not be a knock-off tuxedo, though he can also be witnessed wearing grey sweats for exercise. A cartoonishly simple wardrobe means that most are uncertain whether or not it is only one outfit worn daily, but few would dare to ask him.  

***Personality:***  
The son of a military man and a former crime scene investigator, Anthony is reserved and practical. He rarely speaks more than a handful of words at a time, prioritizing the efficiency of communication over its casual applications. Anthony is intense, rough, and tempered. He seems quick to act because his instincts are honed for reaction, but every movement is a calculated use of his energy.  

When confronted with genuine emotional vulnerability, Anthony's hard demeanor tends to slip – revealing how socially awkward he truly is. Encounters like these tend to be rare for him, though, because he avoids sentimentality as if it were an infectious disease that would poison him.  

He approaches life with an attitude of respect towards those who prove their capabilities. His approval is never freely given, always earned, and not easy to achieve. Anthony values the disciplines of both mind and body, while irritated by gossip and pomposity.
