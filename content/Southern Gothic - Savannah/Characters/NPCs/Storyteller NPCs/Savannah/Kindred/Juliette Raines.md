***Description:***  
Jules is striking in an unconventional way—more emotional firework than cold statue. She’s almost always dressed in stylish thrifted pieces that toe the line between femme punk and faded glamour: sheer blouses over band tees, torn tights with elegant skirts, rings on every finger, and boots that have seen too many clubs and too many streets. Her voice is warm and melodic, with a subtle rasp that deepens when she’s feeling intense. Since her Embrace, her expressions feel more charged, as if every emotion she’s feeling is just a second from spilling over. Her movements still carry human awkwardness—she hasn’t yet found the Daeva grace that her Sire wields so effortlessly—but what she lacks in polish, she makes up for in raw authenticity.

***Personality:***  
Jules is impulsive, sensitive, and utterly incapable of hiding what she feels—something she views as both her greatest strength and most dangerous flaw. She loves fiercely, wounds easily, and bounces between confidence and crushing doubt like a pendulum caught in a storm. While Ash is ice, Jules is fire: passionate, unpredictable, and deeply hungry for connection. She wants to *belong*, to prove herself, and to understand what it means to be Kindred without losing who she was. She’s proud to be Ash’s Childe—even if she doesn’t always understand him. Her loyalty is unshakable, her ambition still forming, and her unlife just beginning.
