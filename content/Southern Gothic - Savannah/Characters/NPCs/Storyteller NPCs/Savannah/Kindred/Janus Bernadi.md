***Description:***   
Janus is an eldest son and heir to the BERNADI BROTHERS SHIPPING COMPANY, specialising in overnight and refrigerated trucking and operating out of the Port of Savannah. Janus is a large Italian-American man of few words, physically hardened by the labor required of the business.   

Janus came to the attention of Oswald Mendoza during the process of renovating the Shattered Refuge, and stayed on his radar as a man who would do deliveries and consult on unusual logistical requests without asking too many questions.   

***Personality:***   
Janus' demeanor is truly chilling. His whole aura has been cultivated to ensure people to know that he is dangerous without him having to explain it. Why waste breath on talking, you know? He is a proud professional, although not particularly ambitious (so far,) as his life's plan was pretty much decided for him by his family.
