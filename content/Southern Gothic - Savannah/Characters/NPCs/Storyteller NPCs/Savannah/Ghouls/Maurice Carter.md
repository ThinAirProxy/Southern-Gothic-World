***Description:***  
Big Mo is a towering, intimidating presence with the calm silence of someone who doesn't need to prove himself. His bright, flame-red beard is his signature, matched by deep-set eyes that miss nothing. Clad in heavy, layered streetwear and worn black jackets, he stands watch like a city gargoyle—always watching, always steady. Though clearly built for violence, his face holds more weariness than cruelty.  

***Personality:***  
Big Mo is quiet, loyal, and utterly unshakable. He speaks sparingly, but when he does, it’s worth listening. His loyalty to Toni comes from their shared past—not from fear, glamour, or blood—and he remains one of the only people who still calls her *Annie*. Mo’s presence grounds the Hive, delivering justice without excess and compassion without softness. He's not here for power. He’s here to protect what he built—and who he believes in.
