***Description:***  
Roux is tall and slender, her pale white skin stretched tight over sharp features. Her eyes are deep brown, solemn and steady, while her long, curly brown hair flows freely around her shoulders. Her features, sharpened into ethereal grace by Galloi blood magic, are haunting—more alien than mortal. Her body appears elongated and unnatural, with distended joints and unsettling proportions. Her hands and fingers especially seem too long, too sharp, too still.  

***Personality:***  
Roux is a creature of contrasts—once a scientist and atheist, now a devoted Hierophant of a forgotten goddess. She is quiet and deeply polite, but speaks with the weight of vision and ritual. She rarely raises her voice, preferring presence and silence to command attention. Unafraid to embrace her monstrous nature, she weaves beauty, fear, and power into every step. A recluse in recent months, she is nonetheless a formidable presence—guarding sacred spaces and unraveling heretics with equal precision.  

***Current Role in Savannah:***  
Roux has arrived in Savannah at the behest of her sire, following a disturbing prophetic vision regarding the Brood’s actions in the city. She brings with her a substantial personal library of magical tomes and relics, and is an expert in blood sorcery—particularly **Cruac**. Her purpose is to investigate the Brood’s blood magic, identify any corruption threatening the city or the Circle of the Crone, and assist in purging it. Roux's presence marks a significant infusion of mystical expertise into Savannah’s occult circles, and her work is already stirring ancient tensions beneath the surface.
